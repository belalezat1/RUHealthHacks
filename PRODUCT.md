# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Operations coordinators monitor consult demand and staffing risk, propose interventions, and route offers for lead approval. Leads review and approve proposals. Physicians respond to offers addressed to them, limited to the relevant hospital context.

## Product Purpose

The Sevaro Command Center helps operations teams detect coverage risk, understand candidate eligibility, and coordinate human-approved staffing interventions. Success means a reviewer can follow an intervention from recommendation through approval and physician response while seeing authoritative outcomes and audit history.

## Positioning

An explainable operations decision desk that connects coverage risk to a human-approved intervention and its outcome.

## Brand Personality

Urgent, decisive, operational. The interface should remain calm enough for high-consequence decisions and explicit about what is known, simulated, stale, or unverified.

## Anti-references

Avoid visual-only workload dashboards, invented eligibility or operational data, and interfaces that present simulated delivery or projected capacity as verified live availability.

## Design Principles

- Show the operational issue and its evidence before presenting an action.
- Keep eligibility, availability, routability, and planned capacity distinct.
- Make approval and physician response explicit human decisions.
- Preserve the authoritative server outcome after races or stale revisions.
- Keep simulation and mock data visibly separate from live operational data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, semantic labels, and responsive layouts for physician mobile views.
